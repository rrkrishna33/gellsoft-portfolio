GellSoft RentPro

Bookings, advances and returns for rental shops, on your phone.

Get it on Google Play

Never double-book an item again. Your data stays on your phone.

GellSoft RentPro runs on your Android phone. Book items for an event, see at a glance what is going out and coming back, take advance and balance payments, and keep photos of the items. It works without an internet connection, and your bookings, customers and photos are saved on your own phone, not on our servers.

What you can do

  • Bookings. Pick the customer and the items, set the event, pickup and return dates, and the booking is saved. The app warns you when an item is already booked for those dates.
  • Alerts before the event. A reminder to send the items a few days before the event, so nothing goes out late.
  • Advance, balance and deposit. Record what the customer paid, what is still due, and the deposit to give back on return.
  • Photos. Take photos of the items when they go out and when they come back.
  • Your items. Costumes, equipment, decor or vehicles, each with its rate, deposit, size and photo. Mark an item as in repair so it cannot be booked.
  • Customers. A list of your customers with their booking history.
  • Today at a glance. Pickups today, returns today, overdue returns, events this week and the money still to collect.
  • English and Tamil. Use the whole app in English or Tamil.

Free and Pro

  • Free: up to 12 new bookings every month and 2 photos on each booking, with items, customers and the dashboard.
  • Pro: unlimited bookings, unlimited photos, and daily or custom reminder notifications for the shop owner. Pro is billed monthly or yearly through Google Play, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

Your data and privacy

Your bookings, customers, items and photos are saved on your phone. The app contacts our license server only to check your plan (Free or Pro). Signing in with Google is optional, and you can delete your account in the app at any time. Read the privacy policy, the terms of service, and how to delete your data.
