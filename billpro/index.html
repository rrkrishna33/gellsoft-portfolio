GellSoft BillPro

A simple, fast billing app for shops and small businesses.

Get it on Google Play

Bill in seconds. Your data stays on your phone.

GellSoft BillPro runs on your Android phone. Make bills in seconds, keep track of stock, purchases and what customers owe, and see how your business is doing. It works without an internet connection, and your bills, customers and products are saved on your own phone, not on our servers.

What you can do

  • Quick billing. Tap or scan items, choose Cash, UPI or Credit, and the bill is done. Scan barcodes with the camera or a scanner gun.
  • GST invoices. Tax invoices with CGST, SGST and IGST, HSN codes, discounts, returns and credit notes, as PDF to print or share.
  • UPI payment QR. Show a QR for the exact bill amount so the customer can pay with any UPI app.
  • Stock and purchases. Track stock, low-stock alerts, units, categories, suppliers and what you owe them.
  • Customers and credit. Customer accounts, statements, payment reminders and balances.
  • Estimates and quotations. Send a quotation, then turn it into an invoice with one tap.
  • Reports. Sales, profit, GST and financial-year reports with charts, and export to CSV.
  • Made for your business. Retail, wholesale, restaurant, services and pharmacy, each with the features that suit it.
  • Thermal printing. Print receipts on Bluetooth thermal printers.
  • English and Tamil. Use the whole app in English or Tamil.

Free and Pro

  • Free: up to 30 bills every month, with quick billing, customers, products and stock.
  • Pro: unlimited bills plus purchases, expenses, profit and GST reports, estimates, customer statements, wholesale prices, labels, reminders and more. A 7-day free trial is included. Pro is billed monthly or yearly through Google Play, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

Your data and privacy

Your bills, customers and products are saved on your phone. The app contacts our license server only to check your plan (Free or Pro). Signing in with Google is optional, and you can delete your account in the app at any time. Read the privacy policy, the terms of service, and how to delete your data.
