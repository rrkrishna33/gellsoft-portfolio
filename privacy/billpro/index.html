Privacy policy

GellSoft BillPro

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Applies to the GellSoft BillPro Android app, published by GellSoft.

In short

Your business data stays on your own phone. We do not collect it, we do not receive it, and the app does not send it anywhere. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics in the app.

The app uses the internet only to check which plan (Free or Pro) your phone has, and to let you subscribe to Pro through Google Play. That check never includes your bills, customers, products or any other business data.

What the app stores on your phone

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your device only:

  • Your business details (name, address, phone, GST number, logo).
  • Your products, stock, purchases, expenses and prices.
  • The customer details you choose to enter (such as name, phone, email, address, GST number) and the bills, returns and payments you record for them.
  • Your settings, including an optional app-lock PIN. The PIN is stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value, never as the PIN itself.

We have no access to this data. If you enter information about your own customers, you are responsible for handling it lawfully as the owner of your business.

Plan check and subscriptions

GellSoft BillPro has a Free plan, a short free trial of Pro, and a paid plan called Pro. To work out which plan your phone has, the app contacts our license server (playstore.gellsoft.com), usually once a day and after a purchase. This is all it sends:

InformationWhy we need it
A random install IDMade by the app the first time it runs, to recognise this installation and keep your plan. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email or Google account.
A scrambled phone IDA one-way code made from your phone's Android ID. The Android ID itself is never sent and cannot be recovered from the code. It is used only so that the free trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app or clearing its data, and so that one subscription is not shared by too many phones.
The app versionTo know whether your app needs an update.
A purchase token (only if you subscribe or restore a purchase)Given to the app by Google Play. Our server uses it only to ask Google whether the subscription is active. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy, not the token itself.

The server answers with a digitally signed license that tells the app whether it is on the free trial, Free or Pro. We also see the ordinary technical details of any internet request, such as the IP address, which we use only to keep the service running and to limit abuse.

We do not receive your business data, your customers' data, your name, your email address or your payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

The app keeps working offline. If the server cannot be reached, the app keeps using the last license it received. The Free plan limits how many bills you can create each month and which features are available. It never locks you out of your own data, which you can always view and export.

The date and time check

Your plan and your monthly bill count depend on the date. The signed license carries the correct time, and the app uses it to check that your phone's date and time has not been changed. A small clock at the top of the app shows the checked time. If the date was changed, the app asks you to correct it before you continue.

To do this the app reads, on your phone only, whether Automatic date & time is on and how long the phone has been running. These readings are not sent to us.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet: only for the plan check and for subscribing to Pro, as described above.
  • Google Play Billing: so you can subscribe to Pro through Google Play.
  • Camera: only to scan barcodes and QR codes. Pictures are processed on your phone to read the code. They are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices: only to connect to your receipt printer and print bills and labels.
  • Fingerprint / biometrics: only if you turn on fingerprint unlock. Android checks your fingerprint. The app never sees or stores it.

Things you start yourself

  • Backups. When you create a backup, the app produces a file and Android's file picker lets you choose where to save it. That file contains your business data, so keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a file you choose.
  • Sharing, printing and exports. When you share an invoice PDF, save a spreadsheet (CSV) export, or print, the file goes to the app, folder or printer you choose. After that, that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Payment reminders. When you tap the reminder button for a customer, the app opens WhatsApp or your messages app with a ready-made message to that customer's phone number. The app does not send anything itself: you press send. The number is passed to that app on your phone at that moment, and nowhere else. After that, WhatsApp's or your phone's own policy applies.
  • Android system backup. If Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android itself may back up app data to your Google account. That is controlled by your phone and Google, not by us.

Third-party components

Barcode scanning uses Google's ML Kit barcode scanner, which runs on your device. Subscriptions use Google Play Billing, which is operated by Google under Google's own privacy policy. The app currently contains no advertising or analytics services.

Security

All communication with our license server uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Licenses are digitally signed, so they cannot be altered on the way. Your PIN is stored only as a scrambled value.

How long we keep information, and deleting it

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. You can also clear it in Android Settings → Apps → GellSoft BillPro → Storage. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Our license server holds only the install ID, the scrambled phone ID, the app version, plan dates and the hashed purchase token. To have that removed, or to ask what we hold, see how to delete your data, or email support@gellsoft.com with the install ID shown on the app's GellSoft Pro screen.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your information, for example if we add advertising or more online features, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
