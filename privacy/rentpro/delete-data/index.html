GellSoft RentPro

Delete your data

For the GellSoft RentPro Android app, published by GellSoft. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Signed in with Google? Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account, read the message and tap Delete account again.
  3. Choose the same Google account you signed in with, to confirm that it is you.

Your email address, name and Google account ID are erased from our server straight away. Your bookings, customers, items and photos stay on your phone: we never received them. Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription; cancel that in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

If you cannot open the app, email support@gellsoft.com from the Google email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my GellSoft RentPro account. We delete it and confirm by reply within 30 days.

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. In the app, open the menu, tap GellSoft Pro, and find Your install ID. Tap the copy button next to it.
  2. Email support@gellsoft.com with the subject Delete my GellSoft RentPro data and paste the install ID in the message.
  3. We reply to confirm, and delete the data described below within 30 days.

If you can no longer open the app, you can still write to us without the install ID. We will tell you what else we need to find your record.

What we delete

The GellSoft license server keeps one small record for each installation of the app. When you ask, we delete all of it:

  • If you signed in with Google: your email address, name and Google account ID.
  • Your random install ID.
  • The scrambled phone ID.
  • The app version and the dates of your free trial.
  • The scrambled (hashed) copy of your subscription's purchase token, and which phones were linked to it.
  • Any free access we gave you by hand, for example for testing.

What we do not have, so cannot delete

Your shop data (items, customers, bookings, photos, settings and backups) is stored only on your own phone. We never receive it. To remove it, uninstall the app, or clear it in Android Settings → Apps → GellSoft RentPro → Storage. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

What may remain, and for how long

  • Google Play records. Your subscription, payment and billing history are held by Google Play, not by us. To stop a subscription, cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. To delete Google's records, use your Google Account settings.
  • Ordinary server logs. Our web server records the technical details of requests (such as the IP address and time) only to keep the service running and to limit abuse. They are not linked to your plan, and they are kept for a limited time and then removed.

What happens after we delete your record

  • The app keeps working. It simply asks the server again, which treats the phone as new.
  • An active subscription stops being recognised on the phone until you tap Restore purchases. Cancel the subscription in the Play Store if you do not want to keep it.
  • Because the free trial is worked out from the phone, deleting your record may let a phone that has used its free trial start a new one.
